What EEATClean does

Procedures change, tools disappear and standards get revised. Your published articles, however, stay the same. One outdated fact is enough to make a buyer doubt the rest of your expertise.

Age is only half the story, though. In our own study, 3 corrections out of 4 were not caused by time: the sentence was already wrong on the day it was published. That is why EEATClean checks every key claim, not only the old ones.

EEATClean reads your articles and lists the claims a reader relies on: versions, prices, dates, standards and figures. Then it checks each claim against current sources and tells you which ones no longer hold, or never did. For each correction, you see the outdated sentence and the link to the source that proves it, so you can check it in one click before you change anything.

It is built for small B2B companies that sell their expertise, such as training, consulting and coaching firms. Agencies and publishers of expert content use it too.

How it works

  1. Add your website. EEATClean reads your sitemap and imports your articles into a content library. It works with any website that has a sitemap, and there is no plugin to install.
  2. Choose the articles to analyze. For each one, EEATClean checks the key claims live against current sources.
  3. Read the verdict. Each article is marked as needing an update, to review or remove, or good to keep. Every correction comes in plain language, with its source.
  4. Keep watching. With Monitoring, your key articles are checked again every month, every 3 months or every 6 months.

No source, no correction. When EEATClean can't find a source that proves an error, it does not propose a change. It also tells you when an article is still right.

What our own study found

Before opening EEATClean to others, we checked 23 of our own articles and 20 pages from other sites. We found 70 corrections in our articles. Surprisingly, only 1 in 4 came from a fact that had changed since publication. The rest were errors from day one, or claims that were never tied to a date.

The sources were clear, too. Out of the 68 corrections that needed one, 62 were proven by the official page of the product's maker or of the public body concerned. In other words, the proof is almost always out there, as long as someone goes and reads it.

Frequently asked questions

What is EEATClean?

EEATClean is a web tool that finds outdated or inaccurate facts in expert articles. It shows each correction next to the source that proves it, and it never edits your site.

Where do the sources come from?

Most often from the official page of the product's maker or of the public body concerned. In our study of 23 articles, 62 of the 68 corrections that needed a source were proven by such a page.

Does EEATClean change my website?

No. It reads your sitemap and your public pages. There is no plugin to install, and you decide what to change.

Which websites does it work with?

Any website that has a sitemap, in any CMS. Articles are analyzed in their own language, and the interface is available in English and French.

How much does it cost?

There is a 7-day trial with 3 deep analyses. Plans start at €49 per month for 25 analyses.
